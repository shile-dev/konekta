Konekta is an offline-first digital wallet with an AI assistant. The core idea is simple: most fintech apps in Nigeria require stable internet to function — but millions of everyday transactions happen in markets, campuses, and areas where connectivity is unreliable. Konekta solves this by letting people send and receive money without internet, using a local device connection.

The AI assistant (powered by Claude) means users do not need to navigate menus or forms. They just type — in plain English or Pidgin — and Konekta understands them.
